Outfit Manager — Concept

RimWorld 1.6 · published (Steam Workshop) · reverse-engineered from existing mod

Vision

A RimWorld mod that makes colonists dress for the job they actually do. Outfit Manager extends the vanilla apparel-scoring algorithm with work-type-aware evaluation, so a miner, a doctor, and a shooter each gravitate toward clothing that genuinely benefits their assigned work — without the player micromanaging every wardrobe.

Target users

  • RimWorld colony managers — players who run multi-pawn colonies and want apparel choices to follow each pawn's work assignment automatically, instead of fighting the vanilla "best raw armor" bias.
  • Optimization-minded players — players who care that a pawn's gear measurably improves their effective output (work speed, relevant stats) and want scoring they can reason about and tune.
  • Mid-to-late-game / large-colony players — players whose colony size makes per-pawn outfit micromanagement impractical and who need the optimizer to scale to many pawns and many apparel items.

Value proposition

Vanilla RimWorld scores apparel mostly on protection and condition, ignoring whether a garment actually helps a pawn do their job. Outfit Manager injects a work-type score into the apparel-scoring pipeline so pawns auto-equip clothing that fits the work they perform — turning outfit selection from blunt armor-maximizing into role-aware optimization, while staying inside the vanilla "Optimize apparel" flow players already know.

Pillars

  • Work-aware scoringApparel is judged by how much it helps the work a pawn is actually assigned to, not by armor alone.
  • Augment, don't replaceHook into vanilla's existing apparel-optimization job rather than building a parallel UI or supplanting the base mechanic.
  • Tunable & legibleScoring is configurable per work type and built to be reasoned about, so players can understand and adjust why a pawn picked a garment.
  • Scales with the colonyStays performant across many pawns and many apparel items so it remains useful into the late game.

Core Identity

A focused, surgical optimization mod — not a sweeping overhaul. Outfit Manager does one thing: it teaches the vanilla apparel optimizer to care about work type. It is pragmatic and quiet, slotting into existing player habits rather than demanding new ones, and it trusts the base game's systems instead of competing with them.

Unique Hook

Work-type score evaluation injected directly into vanilla apparel scoring — pawns dress for their job, automatically, inside the optimizer the game already runs.

Anti-Pillars

  • Not an apparel-content modexplicitly NOT this — it adds no new clothing, materials, or items; it only changes how existing apparel is scored.
  • Not a custom outfit UI replacementexplicitly NOT this — it does not replace the vanilla outfit/apparel-policy interface with a parallel management screen.
  • Not a manual micromanagement toolexplicitly NOT this — the goal is automated, work-aware selection, not more per-pawn dressing chores.
  • Not a total combat/armor overhaulexplicitly NOT this — it does not rebalance vanilla armor or combat mechanics.

Constraints

  • Targets RimWorld 1.6 only (per About/About.xml supported versions).
  • Requires Harmony (brrainz.harmony) and the shared LordKuper.Common library as hard mod dependencies, loaded after both.
  • Core behaviour depends on a Harmony transpiler patching JobGiver_OptimizeApparel.ApparelScoreRaw; vanilla changes to that method are a compatibility risk.
  • Must coexist with vanilla apparel-optimization behaviour and other apparel-scoring mods that touch the same job — the integration surface is shared.
  • Distributed via Steam Workshop under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 for assets (per README.md).
